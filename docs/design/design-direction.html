Mobile: map-first, pills scroll, details in a glass bottom sheet.

Look & feel

  • Doto everywhere (heavier weights 600–900 for legibility)
  • Glass HUD panels over a dark city map with a vignette
  • Teal → sky accent gradient for primary actions; teal glow for selection

New visual elements

  • Fleet pulse: total, status stack bar, trend sparkline
  • Hero vehicle illustration per asset type, signal and GPS badges
  • Ring gauges (battery, range, health) + speedometer arc
  • Energy chart (this session) + events timeline
  • Selected-vehicle trail, callout and compass
  • Vehicle tray: cards for vehicles in view

Data honesty

  • Charts only use data we really have: the session buffer since selection plus the mock trail; no invented history
  • Events come from status transitions and ignition on/off times
  • Parameters stay raw, no units (D-017)
